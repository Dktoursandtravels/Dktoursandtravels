// Who Designs Your Trip

A studio, not an agency.

DK Tours & Travels is based out of Andheri West, Mumbai, and works with a deliberately small number of travellers each season. There is no call centre and no fixed package — each itinerary is drawn from a private brief: who is travelling, what pace suits them, and what "extraordinary" means to that particular traveller.

From a week on the ice in Antarctica to a slow month across the Mediterranean by private yacht, the process stays the same — a single point of contact, considered recommendations, and a plan built entirely around you.

RM
Rituparna Mukhopadhyay Founder & CEO, DK Tours & Travels
